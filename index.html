Safety Guidelines

  • Sanitized & Clean:
    Every unit is deep-cleaned and sanitized before delivery.
  • Adult Supervision:
    An adult must be present at all times to monitor the children.
  • Weather Policy:
    Units cannot be operated in winds exceeding 15mph or heavy rain.
  • No Shoes or Sharp Objects:
    Please remove shoes, jewelry, and ensure no food/drinks inside.
