Safety First Guidelines

Your safety is our top priority. Please review our essential rules and policies.

All our inflatables undergo a rigorous 3-step cleaning and sanitation process before and after every rental to ensure a hygienic environment.

For the safety of all participants, inflatables cannot be operated in winds exceeding 15mph, heavy rain, or lightning. Deposits are fully refundable due to severe weather.

A responsible adult (18+) must constantly supervise the inflatables. No shoes, food, drinks, or sharp objects are permitted inside the bounce areas.
